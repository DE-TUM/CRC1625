CCSS Ontology: Description back to ToC

Ontology for supporting the analysis of Compositionally Complex Solid Solutions (CCSS) in the context of the Collaborative Research Center (CRC) 1625. This ontology models the experimental (meta-)data, provenance and handover workflows present in MatInf instances and vocabulary employed for the analysis of CCSS, while additionally being compatible with existing Materials Science ontologies. This additionally includes the modelling of handover workflow models, and other (meta-)data that is exclusively used by the MatInf semantic layer. Note that some classes are not present in the ontology document. Namely, specific measurement types are dynamically generated, as they change between MatInf instances. Processes and activities for them, however, are included here.

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/AFMProcess

A process where AFM measurements have been performed. Used by Activity instances and Activity Models to denote the type of measurements they refer to. Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM) is a surface characterization technique that maps the topography of a material by scanning a sharp probe across the surface and monitoring the tip–sample interaction forces. It provides nanometer- to atomic-scale height information and can also reveal surface roughness, grain structure, and local mechanical or adhesive contrasts, depending on the measurement mode.

APT Processc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/APTProcess

A process where APT measurements have been performed. Used by Activity instances and Activity Models to denote the type of measurements they refer to. APT (Atom Probe Tomography) is a time-of-flight mass spectrometer that provides three-dimensional (3D) compositional information with sub-nanometer spatial resolution. APT specimens must be sharp and needle-shaped, with typical tip radii below 100 nm to facilitate the field evaporation of surface atoms in the form of ions, thereby enabling their analysis. APT is based on the principle of field evaporation, a process derived historically from field ion microscopy (FIM).

EBSD Processc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/EBSDProcess

EBSD (Electron Backscatter Diffraction) is a method to measure the local orientations of a crystalline specimen from an electron backscatter diffraction pattern. EDX Microscopy Processc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/EDXMicroscopyProcess

A process where EDX measurements have been performed. Used by Activity instances and Activity Models to denote the type of measurements they refer to. During EDX (Energy Dispersive X-Ray Spectroscopy), when the electrons hit the matter of the specimen, several interactions are possible. One is the production of characteristic x-ray, by kicking out an inner shell electron, whose space has to be filled by an outer shell electron. The decrease in energy is emitted as X-ray quant. The energy of the x-rays is characteristic of the element; thus, this technique can be used to calculate the (bulk) composition of the sample. Also called EDS (energy dispersive x-ray Spectroscopy)

Handover workflow modelc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/HandoverWorkflowModel

The beginning of a series of processes that need to be performed. An instance of this entity will contain a two-layer hierarchy of consecutive HandoverWorkflowModelStep instances that correspond to hypothetical handover groups of a given sample. Each step will contain zero or more Activity Model instances that dictate the desired measurements that the sample should contain, among other metadata, and that are taken at the time of any of the handovers within its corresponding handover group. Note that a Handover workflow Model instance dictates the processing steps, but are not linked to a specific samples - they instead act as templates that can be reused at will and assigned to different groups of samples. An entity of type HandoverWorkflowModelInstance will thus completely characterize a HandoverWorkflowModel by being able to apply it to existing objects in the RDMS during validation.

SEM Processc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://crc1625.mdi.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/SEMProcess

A process where SEM measurements have been performed. Used by Activity instances and Activity Models to denote the type of measurements they refer to. In SEM (Scanning Electron Microscopy) an electron beam is rastered across the surface. The interaction with the sample (surface) produces several signals, which can be used for imaging, chemical analysis or analysis of the crystal structure. Secondary electrons and backscattered electrons can be used for image acquisition: the former gives a topography contrast, the latter a z-contrast (z = atomic number). Backscatter diffraction analysis (also see: EBSD) is a technique for crystal structure analysis. X-rays, produced during interaction with the sample, can give information about the chemical composition, usually analyzed using EDX, less commonly but more accurately: WDX.
has super-classes
Analysing Process c, Characterization activity c, Unidentifiable process c

A process where XPS measurements have been performed. Used by Activity instances and Activity Models to denote the type of measurements they refer to. XPS (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy) is a method that uses X-rays to eject electrons from a material and analyzes their energies to identify which elements are present and in what chemical states. It probes only the outermost atomic layers, making it highly surface-specific.
